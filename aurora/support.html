If an alarm failed to ring, tell us the time it was set for, the theme, and whether the phone was locked, in Focus, or in Low Power Mode. That combination is almost always the answer.

If an alarm did not ring

Work through these in order. In our experience the cause is in the first two more than nine times out of ten.

1. Notifications
iOS Settings → Notifications → Aurora. Allow Notifications must be on, with Sounds enabled and Time Sensitive allowed.
2. Focus and Sleep
A Sleep Focus that silences Aurora will silence Aurora. Add it to the allowed list, or let Time Sensitive notifications break through.
3. Volume
Aurora rings through the silent switch, but the ringer volume still sets the ceiling. Raise it before bed.
4. Low Power Mode
Low Power Mode can delay background work. The alarm itself is scheduled with the system and still fires, but keep the phone charging overnight if you can.

Other questions

The same song keeps coming back.
A theme with few tracks repeats sooner. Switching theme resets the rotation.
Restoring a purchase
Settings inside Aurora → Restore Purchases, signed in to the Apple Account that made the purchase.
Deleting your data
There is nothing held anywhere but your device. Deleting the app removes every alarm and setting. See the privacy policy.
