Aurora — Privacy Policy

Last updated 10 September 2026

Aurora is a music alarm clock designed to work entirely on your device.

Data we collect

Aurora collects no personal data. Your alarms, themes and settings are stored only on your device and in your device's standard backups. We have no servers, no accounts, no analytics and no advertising.

Apple Music

If you choose an Apple Music playlist as a wake-up source, Aurora asks permission to access Apple Music through Apple's MusicKit. Playback and your library are handled by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy. Aurora never transmits your listening data anywhere, and the feature requires an active Apple Music subscription.

Notifications

Alarms use local notifications scheduled on your device. Nothing leaves your phone in order for an alarm to ring.

Purchases

Aurora Premium subscriptions and the Lifetime purchase are processed entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details. Subscriptions auto-renew unless cancelled in your Apple Account settings at least 24 hours before the end of the period.

Your rights

Since we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to export or delete. Deleting the app removes all local data.

Terms of use

Aurora uses Apple's standard Licensed Application End User License Agreement.
