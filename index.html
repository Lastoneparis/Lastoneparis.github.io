An independent studio building for iPhone, iPad and Mac. Different problems, different apps — held together by how they are built, not by a shared template.

How everything here is built

  • On the device. The work happens on your hardware. Most of these apps have no server at all, which is why most of them work in airplane mode.
  • No account. Almost nothing here asks you to sign up. There is no profile to create and none to delete.
  • No analytics, no third-party SDKs. We do not measure you, and there is no advertising network reading your behaviour except where an app says plainly that it shows ads.
  • Honest about limits. Every app's page says what it cannot do, not only what it can. That section is written before the marketing one.
