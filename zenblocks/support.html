Common questions

My game disappeared.
The board is saved after every placement. If a saved game is genuinely gone, write to us with roughly when you last played — that helps us find it in the logs you send.
Ads still show after I paid.
Settings → Restore Purchases, then relaunch the app. Make sure you are signed in to the Apple Account that made the purchase. If it still shows ads, email us the purchase date.
Can I move my score to a new phone?
The best score is stored on the device and travels in an encrypted iPhone backup. There is no account, so it does not sync between two phones in use at once.
The pieces will not fit anywhere.
Then the game is over — that is the only way ZenBlocks ends. Start a new board; your best score is kept.
How do I delete my data?
Delete the app. Everything it stored goes with it; there is no server copy. See the privacy policy.
