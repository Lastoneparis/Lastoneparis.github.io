No timer. No lives.
No connection.

ZenBlocks is a block puzzle you can play on a plane, in a tunnel, or anywhere else the signal gives up. The board waits for you.

How to play

  1. Take one of the three piecesYou are always offered three. Drag any of them onto the board.
  2. Fill a row or a columnA complete line clears and gives you the space back. Clear more than one at a time and the score compounds.
  3. Keep room for what comes nextNew pieces only arrive once all three are placed. The whole game is leaving yourself somewhere to put them.

There is no move counter and nothing runs out. A game ends only when none of the three pieces will fit — which is a puzzle you lost, not a clock you failed.

What it does not do

  • No countdown Think for as long as you want. Put the phone down mid-game and come back tomorrow.
  • No lives or energy Nothing gates how much you play, and nothing refills on a timer.
  • No account No sign-in, no profile, no friend list. Your board and your best score stay on the device.
  • No connection Every part of the game works in airplane mode.

Questions

Can I undo a placement?
Yes. The last placement can be taken back, so a misdrag does not cost you the game.
Does my game survive quitting the app?
Yes. The board is saved as you play and is exactly where you left it when you return.
What does the purchase remove?
Advertising, permanently. The puzzle itself is complete without it — nothing about the game is gated behind a payment.
I bought it and switched phones.
Settings → Restore Purchases, on the same Apple Account.
