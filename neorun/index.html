Score by the margin,
not the distance

Neorun 3D is a three-lane runner through a synthwave city that pays you for how close you dare to pass — not for how long you survive.

The rule

Running down the middle of an empty lane earns you nothing. Every point in Neorun comes from a graze: passing an obstacle close enough that the game registers the margin. The tighter the gap, the higher the multiplier, and the multiplier is what compounds.

Scoring

PassMarginMultiplier
ClearWide of the obstacle×1
CloseInside the warning band×3
GrazePaint-scraping×6
OverdriveHeld across a full chain×9

A collision does not just end the run — it drops the chain. Overdrive is a state you hold, and the whole game is the tension between banking a chain and pushing it one obstacle further.

Controls

One thumb, portrait, no tilt. It is built to be played standing on a train with the other hand holding on.

Off the grid

No account, no sign-in, no network needed. Scores, prisms and unlocked skins live on your device. Ads are never shown while you are offline, and Remove Ads Forever retires them permanently.

Questions

Does it need an internet connection?
No. Every mode runs offline. The only thing the network is used for is serving ads in the free version.
What are prisms for?
Prisms are earned by running and spent on ship skins. They are not sold for money — there is no currency purchase in the game.
I bought Remove Ads and reinstalled.
Open Settings inside the game and choose Restore Purchases. Apple restores it to any device on the same Apple Account.
My score did not save.
Scores write at the end of a run. Force-quitting mid-run discards that run by design. If a completed run is missing, tell us.
