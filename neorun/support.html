Common fixes

  1. A purchase is missing after reinstalling. Settings → Restore Purchases, on the same Apple Account that bought it.
  2. The game stutters. Close other apps and check Low Power Mode — the city is rendered in 3D and Low Power Mode caps the frame rate.
  3. Ads appear after buying Remove Ads. Restore Purchases, then relaunch. If they persist, write to us with the date of purchase.
  4. No sound. Check the silent switch and Settings → Sound inside the game; the game respects the hardware switch.

Reporting a bug

The most useful report is the one that says what you did, what happened, and what you expected instead. Screenshots or a screen recording of the run are welcome and usually decisive.

Data and deletion

Everything the game knows about you is on your device. Deleting the app deletes all of it — there is no server copy and no account to close. See the privacy policy.
