Neorun 3D — Privacy Policy

LAST UPDATED 10 SEPTEMBER 2026

Neorun 3D is an endless runner designed to be played anywhere, offline.

Data we collect

Neorun 3D itself collects no personal data. Your scores, prisms, unlocked skins and settings are stored only on your device and in your device's standard backups. We have no servers and no accounts.

Advertising

The free version may show ads served by Google AdMob. When ads are shown, Google may collect device identifiers and usage data to serve and measure them, as described in Google's Privacy Policy and AdMob's data practices. On iOS you are asked for tracking permission (App Tracking Transparency); if you decline, ads are non-personalised. Where required, for example in the EEA and the UK, a consent form is shown before any ad is served.

Purchasing Remove Ads Forever removes all advertising, after which no ad-related data is processed at all. Ads are never shown while your device is offline.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details.

Children

Neorun 3D does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ad serving follows Google's policies where applicable.

Your rights

We hold no data about you, so there is nothing for us to export or delete. Deleting the app removes all local data. For ad-related choices see adssettings.google.com.

Terms of use

Neorun 3D uses Apple's standard Licensed Application End User License Agreement.
